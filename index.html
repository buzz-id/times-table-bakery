Times Table Bakery

A times sum is a tray of cookies: 3 × 4 means 3 rows of 4. A sharing sum is cookies on plates: 12 ÷ 3 means 12 shared by 3. Bake every day, earn stickers, then play Cookie Rush.

    The giant tray

    3 × 4 = 12 3 rows of 4 cookies

    Tap any spot on the tray, up to 20 × 20. The cookies fill every row and column up to it. The dashed lines mark every 5, so big trays are easier to count. Try finding all the trays that make 24.

    For grown-ups

    Five minutes a day

    • There are two daily puzzles, Times and Sharing. Each has 4 short steps and earns its own sticker. The same date and level always give the same puzzle, so siblings can compare.
    • Read "×" aloud as "rows of" or "groups of". Read "÷" as "shared by". Say "12 shared by 3" before you say "12 divided by 3".
    • Start with Times on 2, 5 and 10. Add Sharing once the times bake feels easy. Move up a level when both feel easy two days running.
    • Wrong answers just fade out. Let your child try again. Point at the rows or plates rather than giving the answer.
    • Share real things: grapes onto plates, cards to players. Find arrays at home: egg cartons, muffin tins, window panes.
    • Cookie Rush starts in No timer mode: 10 questions, no clock and no speed points. Switch to 60 seconds once your child feels confident. In both modes points never go down, and a missed fact comes back later in the same round.
    • Stickers, the streak and best scores are saved in this browser on this device only.
